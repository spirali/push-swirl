# Privacy Policy for "Push & Swirl"

**Last Updated:** May 31, 2026

> **In plain terms:** This app does not collect, transmit, or share any data about you or your device. Everything stays on your phone.
 
---

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application **Push & Swirl** (the "Application") for mobile devices.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
The Application is designed to be a simple training timer. 

* **No Personal Data:** The Application does not collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location).
* **Local Storage Only:** Any training times, intervals, or settings you create are stored strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized with any external servers.

### 2. Internet and Connectivity
The Application operates entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection, does not contain advertisements, and does not communicate with any third-party analytics or online services.

### 3. Open Source Transparency
The Application is open-source software. You can independently audit the source code, verify how data is handled, or contribute to the project at our public repository: https://github.com/spirali/push-swirl.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Because the Application does not collect any personal data from any users, it is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations, including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us via email at **ada@kreatrix.org** or open an issue on our public repository.
